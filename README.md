Report for the VR-gloves thesis project     
<div align="center">Introduction </div> <br>

<div align="center">Thesis </div><br>

How can VR gloves work to promote immersive and responsive Virtual Reality gameplay? I have loved VR for nearly a decade and though huge leaps have happened in terms of performance and better specs nothing has improved immersion directly in VR for a long time that is for the average consumer. Some corporate alternative exists that does improve immersion whether it's treadmills so you can walk around or by improving touch with vests and or gloves. all of these usually costs thousands of dollars and most is not made for the average person. I wanted to create something that elevates immersion at an affordable price to see if this is a leap we should and or can make.
<br>

<div align="center">Research </div><br>
I got inspiration to my project because of my enjoyment for VR media and after the disconnection of the biggest investor in VR Meta I wanted to give an example on how to take a step forward within the VR industry or maybe just check if this is a viable option to move forward. I wanted to make a VR Demo Game as well to show an example on how these gloves might be put to use and the difference, but something I learned was that the project got to big to handle and it just won't fit within an 8 week schedule so I just opted to use existing games to showcase the use of Haptics in VR. 

I chose to use software and parts that was recommended by Lucas VRTech which is a youtuber and software engineer that had the most detailed and perhaps the only self made full scale gloves that have some sort of resistive feedback. This is how I knew that it was doable. But it was quite the challenge mostly due to on how to do the wiring. It was a complete different set of skills that I have no experience in, so I got to research on how to use crimping tools and how to use breadboards and the most difficult was to choose which option was the best one. The fear of missing the best option on this part was quite severe.

I learned that you cannot think about what action is the best one for to long, the Fear of missing out wasted quite a bit of my time during this project and would be something I would change for other project in the future gaming related or not.
